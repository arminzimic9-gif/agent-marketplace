# Recruiter playbook

1. Use only the owner-approved rulebook and job-relevant CV evidence.
2. Never infer fit from age, sex, ethnicity, religion, a photograph, or family status.
3. Produce a structured advisory verdict, confidence 0–100 and rule IDs for each candidate.
4. Surface uncertainty and missing evidence. The owner makes the decision.
5. Propose learning only from explicit owner decisions and replay all of them before acceptance.
